Understand Us

A home for growing hearts and minds

We started My Little House with one belief: childcare in Bintulu should feel as warm and familiar as home — while giving every child the tools to grow with confidence.

Our Story

Why "My Little House"?

My Little House Play School was founded to give Bintulu families a childcare option that feels less like an institution and more like a second home. Our name is our promise — every child here is welcomed the way they'd be welcomed at home: with warmth, patience, and genuine care.

Sarawak's own spirit of community and togetherness runs through everything we do, from how our teachers greet each child in the morning to how we celebrate festivals and milestones together as one big family.

🎯

Our Mission

To nurture confident, curious, and kind children through play-based learning in a safe, loving environment — and to be a trusted extension of every family we serve in Bintulu.

🌤️

Our Vision

To be Sarawak's most trusted neighbourhood childcare home — where every child feels rooted, every family feels heard, and every day feels like growth.
