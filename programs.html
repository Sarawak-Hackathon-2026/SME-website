Our Curriculum

Learning through play

Playing allows children to learn. As they explore, take risks, use their imaginations, and solve problems while playing, they build vital skills that help them develop socially, physically, and cognitively.

🧭

Self-Direction

The child has complete control over what and how they play. Adults supervise and may suggest, but the child leads.

🔍

Unstructured Exploration

Children are free to explore and choose activities based on their own preferences, from a variety of choices.

😄

Fun

Play should be pleasurable. Children are never forced into an activity — enjoying it is what matters.

🌀

Process-Oriented

The process is what matters, not a perfect result or a fixed end goal.

What we look for in every room

Every classroom looks a little different, but high-quality environments share the same standards:

🕊️ Soothing environment — calm lighting, uncluttered spaces

🗺️ Defined areas — separate zones for sleeping, playing, eating, and changing

🤸 Secure open spaces — firm surfaces for movement and exploring

🏠 Personal touches — familiar objects and photos from home

🧩 Simple, interesting materials — open-ended toys in varied sizes and textures

🪑 Child-size furniture — sturdy, safe, and the right scale for little ones
