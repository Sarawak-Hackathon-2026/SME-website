Our Curriculum

Learning through play

Playing allows children to learn. As they explore, take risks, use their imaginations, and solve problems while playing, they build vital skills that help them develop socially, physically, and cognitively.

🧭

Self-Direction

The child has complete control over what and how they play. Adults supervise and may suggest, but the child leads.

🔍

Unstructured Exploration

Children are free to explore and choose activities based on their own preferences, from a variety of choices.

😄

Fun

Play should be pleasurable. Children are never forced into an activity — enjoying it is what matters.

🌀

Process-Oriented

The process is what matters, not a perfect result or a fixed end goal.

What we look for in every room

Every classroom looks a little different, but high-quality environments share the same standards:

🕊️ Soothing environment — calm lighting, uncluttered spaces

🗺️ Defined areas — separate zones for sleeping, playing, eating, and changing

🤸 Secure open spaces — firm surfaces for movement and exploring

🏠 Personal touches — familiar objects and photos from home

🧩 Simple, interesting materials — open-ended toys in varied sizes and textures

🪑 Child-size furniture — sturdy, safe, and the right scale for little ones

🧗 Small physical challenges — safe ways to climb, balance and test new skills

Frequently asked questions

What ages does My Little House Play School take?

We care for children from 3 months to 9 years old: infant daycare (3–18 months), toddler playschool (18–24 months), preschool (3–6 years, split into a 3–4 group and a 5–6 group), and transit care for school-age children aged 7–9.

Where is My Little House Play School located?

We are at No. 40, Grand Height, Jalan Johari Sunam, 97000 Bintulu, Sarawak, Malaysia.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Friday, 7:30am to 6:00pm.

What is the teacher-to-child ratio for infants?

Our infant room runs at one teacher to every three babies.

Do you offer a programme for children with additional needs?

Yes. Our inclusive programme provides early-intervention activities for children with needs such as mild autism or speech delay. It is not a therapy service and we do not guarantee specific outcomes; suitability is discussed with each family individually.

What kind of curriculum do you follow?

A play-based curriculum built on four ideas: self-direction, unstructured exploration, fun, and a focus on the process rather than the product.

How much are the fees?

Monthly fees are RM 595–650 for full-day care and RM 400–450 for half-day, with a lower rate for children over 2. One-time enrolment costs are a RM 150 registration fee (first time only), RM 180 miscellaneous, and RM 220 for food per half-year. See the full fee breakdown below, or message Ms Siti for a quote for your child's age group.

How do I enrol or arrange a visit?

Message or call Ms Siti on WhatsApp at +60 14-887 9930, Monday to Friday, or send an enquiry through our Contact page. You are welcome to drop by for a tour any weekday.
